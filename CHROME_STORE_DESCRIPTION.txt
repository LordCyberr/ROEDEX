ROEDEX: The Ultimate Real-Time Companion & Tactical Overlay for Roots of Embervault

ROEDEX is an advanced, high-performance, non-intrusive companion overlay engineered exclusively for Roots of Embervault. Designed for both competitive grinders and adventurous explorers, ROEDEX seamlessly integrates directly into your game window to deliver live telemetry, market intelligence, geospatial pathfinding, and efficiency analytics without ever breaking your immersion.
